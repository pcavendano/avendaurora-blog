Bio:

Born in Veracruz, Mexico, and based in Montreal for more than two decades, I have dedicated my life to perfecting the culinary arts. My training combines the rich heritage of Mexican cuisine with the refined techniques of French gastronomy that I acquired at Montreal's finest schools.

My career has taken me from fine-dining restaurants like the Ritz-Carlton Montreal and the Fairmont Mont-Tremblant, to residences where I cook with all my heart for those who need it most. Every dish I prepare tells a story, every ingredient has a purpose.

----

Quote: Cooking is love made visible. Every recipe is a love letter to the people around us.

Philosophy-title: My Culinary Philosophy

----

Philosophy:

I strongly believe in using fresh, local ingredients, in reducing food waste, and in creating dishes that nourish both body and soul. The Mexican cuisine I prepare honors the traditional recipes of my homeland while incorporating modern techniques and Quebec products.

Every recipe on this site is an invitation into my kitchen, a piece of my story shared with you.
